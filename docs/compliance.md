# Record checks and their limits

Checked 13 November 2026. These checks flag missing evidence and local workflow exceptions. They do not certify legal compliance or decide whether a retailer deduction is valid.

## Retailer rules

A deduction needs a deadline and the source terms entered by the operator. There is no universal deadline in this software. The demo dates are fictional local agreements. The evidence policy requires an invoice plus delivery evidence for shortage, inspection evidence for damage, and an agreement for pricing, promotion and other deductions. These are configurable business controls, not statutory rules.

TrewUp describes deduction investigation, exception categorisation and distributor data at https://www.trewup.com/features and exports at https://www.trewup.ai/blog/coming-soon-kroger-data-in-trewup. The desk records an external submission receipt only after an operator actually submits it. It never submits disputes, collects debts, initiates payments or changes accounting entries.

## Personal information in supporting records

NZ Privacy Act principle 5 requires reasonable safeguards for personal information. The SECURITY_EVIDENCE check flags a missing recorded access review for NZ counterparties. A reference is evidence to review, not proof that access controls work. Source: https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/5/

NZ principle 9 limits retention to the period needed for a lawful purpose. RETENTION_REVIEW flags absent or overdue review dates. The software never deletes records automatically; an authorised retention change must cover database history, evidence originals and backups. Source: https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/9/

For Australian entities subject to the Privacy Act, APP 11 covers reasonable security steps and destruction or de-identification when no longer required, subject to exceptions. The same evidence checks run for AU records. The review date is the operator's policy date, not a statutory retention period. Source: https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines/chapter-11-app-11-security-of-personal-information

OTHER jurisdiction records receive no legal privacy check. Confirm applicable rules before use. Generated documents may include private business records. Keep outputs local or in access-controlled storage.
